ITthute Fetch AudioVid alpha20 Picture Failure Analysis / alpha21 Remediation
===============================================================================

Observed during alpha20 testing:
- Normal video downloads completed successfully.
- Facebook and Instagram picture jobs completed with zero picture files.
- A supplied Facebook example used a redirect-style /share/p/... URL.
- Website cookies were not configured/enabled in the captured tests.
- gallery-dl was installed successfully as 1.32.9, although Diagnostics displayed "unknown".

Application defects found:
1. Alpha20 picture workers wrote extractor output to pictures-<job>.log, but the shared progress poller still tailed download-<job>.log. This hid the useful extractor error and caused an empty TMD_LOG_TAIL.
2. Facebook share links were passed directly to gallery extraction rather than first being canonicalised to a post/photo/permalink URL.
3. Authentication-required social-media responses were not distinguished clearly from ordinary zero-image results.
4. gallery-dl version detection was not persisted into the preference read by Diagnostics.
5. The LinkedIn best-effort extractor did not explicitly detect login/authwall/checkpoint responses.

Alpha21 remediation:
- Follow LOG= from the job status and expose the true picture extractor log tail.
- Canonicalise Facebook share/redirect URLs before extraction.
- Reuse imported Netscape cookies in Facebook resolution/extraction when enabled.
- Add platform-specific authentication, access, rate-limit, unsupported-URL and zero-image guidance.
- Detect LinkedIn login/authwall/checkpoint responses.
- Persist gallery-dl version information after dependency verification.

The optional exact-path termux-media-scan timeout seen elsewhere in the supplied Diagnostics is not the root cause of the picture extraction failure: picture jobs produced no files, while Android's primary MediaScannerConnection continued to complete successfully for normal video jobs.

External providers can change authentication/page behavior independently of the app, so fresh browser cookies may still be required for old/restricted posts. Alpha21 makes such failures visible in the job log for precise follow-up remediation.
